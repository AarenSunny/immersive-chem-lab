Impossible Labs

Mixed-reality labs for things that are too dangerous, too expensive, too big or too small to try for real. Each one has a live AI "living world" generated by Visko Orbis.

On Quest: tap Enter, then poke a glowing ball to step into a space. Pinch to grab things and turn your wrist to tilt them. White = home, red = reset, green = living world, purple = zoom view, blue = move the lab.

Desktop preview: click the buttons and drag objects with the mouse. While holding something, scroll (or press Q/E) to tilt it, e.g. to pour.

Create a training space

Describe a place that's too dangerous, expensive, big, small or far away for students to visit. Visko Orbis generates it live, and students work through your steps in mixed reality.
